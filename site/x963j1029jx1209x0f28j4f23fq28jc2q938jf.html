Sign in once in a new tab

The terminal asks for a password before it gives you a shell, and browsers refuse to show that prompt inside a frame — so the first sign-in has to happen in a tab of its own.

  1. Open the terminal in a new tab.
  2. Enter the terminal password, then your Pi login when the shell asks.
  3. Come back here — the browser remembers the credentials for pi.oliverbar.net, so it loads inline from then on.
