    Why go experimental

    Be first to everything.

    Everything stable ULTRA has, plus whatever's next — and a direct hand in what makes it into the public build.

    Fixes land here first

    Crash fixes reach experimental before stable. The audio-bars crash and the "two copies on one profile" bug were both fixed here first.

    New builds, constantly

    A fresh build goes out whenever ULTRA changes — often several in one day — so new features reach you in hours, not weeks.

    Updates itself

    No re-downloading. On Windows, Settings → updates → update now installs the latest build in place.

    Runs alongside stable

    Experimental keeps its own profile, so your regular ULTRA tabs, settings and history stay exactly as they are. Keep both installed.

    Shape what ships

    Testers' bug reports go straight into the next build. What you find here is what gets fixed before everyone else sees it.

    Nothing taken away

    Tiling panes, workspaces, sleeping tabs, ad and tracker blocking, the private workspace — all of stable ULTRA is here too.
